# Audit And Replay

Proof-of-Care is designed so that governance decisions can be inspected after the fact and evaluated before policy changes go live.

## Audit

Audit is not treated as an afterthought. Decision history matters because it preserves:

- the requested action
- the governing conditions
- the decision outcome
- the execution result
- the evidence chain for later review

That record supports technical evaluation, debugging, and governance credibility.

## Replay

Where the decision surface is deterministic, policy changes can be tested against prior decision histories before activation. This matters because governance changes should be evaluated on real behavior patterns rather than intuition alone.

## Why It Matters

Without replay, policy evolution is guesswork.

Without audit, trust claims are weak.

Proof-of-Care aims to make both surfaces structurally available.
